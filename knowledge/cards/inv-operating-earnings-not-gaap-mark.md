# Operating earnings ≠ GAAP mark

Buffett’s 2024 letter restates a measurement choice: communicate the
earnings of controlled businesses, not the yearly swing in securities
prices. That is a **reporting discipline**, not a claim that marks are
unreal.

**Agent use:** in diligence memos, create two lines — (1) operating /
owner earnings of the business, (2) price movement of any residual
portfolio. Never blend them into one “earnings beat.”

**Open question:** this letter does not publish a full owner-earnings
bridge. Do not invent one.
