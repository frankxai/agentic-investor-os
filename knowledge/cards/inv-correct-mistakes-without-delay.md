# Correct errors; do not nurse them

The 2024 letter opens with mistakes in (a) future economics of purchased
businesses and (b) manager ability/fidelity. The named failure is delay.

**Agent use:** risk registers need a “kill/correct” field. “Watching it”
without a trigger is non-compliant with this card.

**Not implied:** that selling is always the correction. The correction is
*action*, which may be recapitalize, replace, write down, or exit —
human-gated.
