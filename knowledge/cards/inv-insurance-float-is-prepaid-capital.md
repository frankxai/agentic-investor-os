# Float is prepaid, not free

Float is capital held because claims are paid later. The letter’s own
caveat is reserving for surprises (it points at catastrophe risk).

**Agent use:** “float” in a memo must be paired with underwriting result
and reserve uncertainty. A positive spread that ignores tails is a failed
card application.

Educational description only. No underwriting conclusion.
