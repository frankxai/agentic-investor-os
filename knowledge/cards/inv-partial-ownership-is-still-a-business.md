# Equities as partial ownership

The letter treats marketable stocks and wholly-owned companies as the
same *kind* of judgment (future economics of a business), with different
control rights.

**Agent use:** thesis briefs must say what the business does, who pays,
and what can break. Chart patterns are not diligence.

**Limit:** 2024 names (Apple, American Express, Coca-Cola, Moody’s) are
*examples in a letter*, not a recommended basket. Do not copy them into
a model book.
