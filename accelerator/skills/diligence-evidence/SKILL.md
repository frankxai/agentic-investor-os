# Diligence Evidence

## Trigger

Screening proceeds, a diligence question is assigned, or evidence coverage must be assessed.

## Evidence states

`observed`, `sourced`, `founder_asserted`, `inferred`, `contradicted`, `stale`, `unknown`.

## Procedure

1. Define the decision question and predeclared evidence bar.
2. Decompose it into market, product, technical, team, GTM, economics, and relevant regulated-review questions.
3. Assign each question an owner, source plan, budget, and stop condition.
4. Prefer primary sources; record URL/location, observed time, excerpt/claim, state, confidence, and limitations.
5. Keep facts, assertions, inferences, and opinions separate.
6. Record contradictions rather than averaging them away.
7. Stop when the evidence bar or time/budget cap is reached.
8. Produce coverage, unresolved risks, falsifiers, and IC-readiness.
9. Memo Editor may synthesize only verified records and clearly labeled unknowns.
10. Route the packet to Independent Verifier and Governance Sentinel.

## Output

Question graph, evidence records, coverage report, risk register, and IC packet draft.

## Gates

Founder/customer/reference outreach, private repos/data rooms, paid sources, security testing, legal review, and public claims require approval.

## Verification

Claims link to evidence IDs. Sources include timestamps. Missing evidence is visible. The artifact contains no autonomous accept/invest conclusion.
