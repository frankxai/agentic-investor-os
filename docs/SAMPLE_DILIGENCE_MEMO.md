# Sample Diligence Memo

This sample uses fictional placeholder data and demonstrates structure only. It does not evaluate a real investment opportunity.

## Opportunity

- Name: Example AI Workflow Company
- Category: Agentic workflow software
- Stage: Placeholder
- Source: Sample intake
- Decision owner: Human reviewer

## Thesis Fit

The opportunity appears aligned with the thesis if it can prove repeatable workflow adoption, measurable customer outcomes, and a defensible distribution path.

## Evidence Queue

| Question | Evidence Needed | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Who is the urgent buyer? | Customer interviews and pipeline notes. | Needed |
| What outcome is measurable? | Before/after workflow metrics. | Needed |
| Is distribution repeatable? | Channel data and conversion history. | Needed |
| What is technically differentiated? | Product walkthrough and architecture notes. | Needed |
| What are the main legal or compliance issues? | Qualified expert review. | Needed |

## Risk Register

| Risk | Why It Matters | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer urgency unclear | Weak urgency can make sales slow and expensive. | Customer evidence |
| Differentiation unproven | Similar workflow products may be easy to copy. | Product and technical review |
| Regulated claims | Customer-facing claims may need review. | Legal/compliance review |
| Data privacy | Workflow systems may process sensitive customer data. | Security and privacy review |

## Open Questions

- What specific workflow does the buyer already budget for?
- What is the activation event?
- What evidence shows the product is used repeatedly?
- Which claims require legal or compliance review?
- What support could materially improve the company in 30 days?

## Human Review Gate

This memo is incomplete until a qualified human reviewer verifies evidence, resolves regulated questions, and approves any decision or communication.
