# Commercial Strategy

## Public Starter

The public repo builds trust through templates, workflows, examples, and clear safety boundaries.

## Paid Offers

| Offer | Buyer | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Diligence Sprint | Angel, fund, accelerator, founder. | Memo, risk register, evidence queue. |
| Portfolio Support Sprint | Portfolio operator or founder. | 30-day support plan and cadence. |
| Investor Update System | Founder or fund. | Update template, review process, communication cadence. |
| Data Room OS | Founder raising capital. | Organized data room structure and checklist. |
| Pro Plugin | Power user or team. | Private workflows, deeper templates, and automation. |

## Boundary

The product sells structure, speed, memo quality, support cadence, and evidence discipline. It does not sell regulated decisions or outcome certainty.
