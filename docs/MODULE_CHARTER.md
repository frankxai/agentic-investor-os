# Module Charter

## Module

- Name: Agentic Investor OS
- ID: `investor-os`
- Standard: Agentic Operating System Standard
- Compliance target: `L3-agent-os`
- Visibility: public starter

## Promise

Agentic Investor OS helps investors, founders, accelerators, and portfolio operators turn opportunity context into diligence memos, risk registers, support plans, and communication packets.

## Public Boundary

Public:

- Templates.
- General workflows.
- Public-safe examples.
- Standard alignment.

Private:

- Live deals.
- Founder data.
- LP data.
- Confidential financials.
- Private memos.
- Internal decision records.
- Proprietary scoring or routing.

## Approval Gates

- Investment decision.
- Legal review.
- Tax/accounting review.
- Securities/compliance review.
- Confidential data.
- Public communication.
- Outreach.

## Proof Artifact

The first proof artifact is a public-safe sample diligence memo.
